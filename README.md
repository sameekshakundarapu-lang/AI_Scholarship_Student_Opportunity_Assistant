# 🎓 AI Scholarship & Student Opportunity Assistant

An AI-powered web application that helps students discover scholarships, internships, hackathons, competitions, fellowships, and other opportunities based on their academic profile, skills, interests, and preferences.

## 📌 About the Project

The AI Scholarship & Student Opportunity Assistant is designed to make opportunity discovery easier for students.

Students can enter their profile details such as education, branch, year of study, CGPA, family income, category, state, skills, and areas of interest. The system then analyzes available opportunities and provides personalized recommendations based on the student's profile.

The application also uses AI to provide simple explanations for why an opportunity may be suitable for the student.

## ✨ Features

- 👩‍🎓 Student profile management
- 🎯 Personalized opportunity recommendations
- 📊 Opportunity matching based on student profile
- 💰 Scholarship recommendations
- 💼 Internship recommendations
- 🏆 Hackathon and competition recommendations
- 🤝 Fellowship recommendations
- 🤖 AI-generated opportunity explanations
- 🔍 Opportunity filtering
- 📱 Simple and user-friendly Streamlit interface
